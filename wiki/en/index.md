Mira is a free [Jellyfin](https://jellyfin.org) client for Windows, Mac and iPhone. Jellyfin keeps your films and series on a computer of yours and serves them; Mira is the pleasant way to watch them. No Mira account, no ads, and nothing leaves your server.

## Pick your device

| Device | What it needs | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Windows | Windows 10 (2004 or later) or 11, x64 | [Install on Windows](windows.md) |
| Mac | Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 15 or later | [Install on Mac](mac.md) |
| iPhone and iPad | iOS 26 or later, AltStore Classic, a free Apple ID | [Install on iPhone](iphone.md) |

There is no Mira for Android for now. Mira web, which Jellyfin used to serve to phones in the browser, ended with 0.9.4.

## What you need

- **A Jellyfin server, 10.9 or later**, on a computer that stays on, and an account on it. No server yet? Mira for Windows installs Jellyfin and sets it up for you: see [Set up Jellyfin](jellyfin.md).
- **Your films and series** in Jellyfin's folders, named the way it recognises them.
- **To watch away from home**: Tailscale, free. Mira has it built in, so most devices need nothing more. See [Away from home](away.md).

## The short version

1. On the computer that will hold your videos, install Mira for Windows and choose **No server yet: Mira installs Jellyfin on this PC**.
2. Put your videos in the **Movies**, **Shows** and **Anime** folders it creates.
3. Open the guide (<kbd>F1</kbd>) and click **Set everything up** on the **Mira on your iPhone** card.
4. Install Mira on your other devices and sign in with the same account.
5. Send your [party link](together.md) to a friend and watch together.

> [!NOTE]
> Mira is a preview: it is used every day, but some things have not been tried on every setup yet (HDR, multichannel audio, several monitors). Mira is an independent project, not affiliated with Jellyfin, and provides no films.
