Your Jellyfin library, like at the movies.

Mira turns your films and series into a real cinema: big artwork, picking up at the right minute, the next episode rolling on its own. On your PC, your Mac and your phone.

Download for Windows Mac, iPhone and Android

FreeOpen sourceNo account

00:01 · On your computer

A cinema in a window.

Mira for Windows and Mac plays everything itself, with the mpv engine: almost any file, as it is.

  1. Home

    Beautiful from the start.

    Posters, large artwork, summaries, cast and sagas: your library looks like a streaming service, with nothing to set up.

  2. Resume

    Pick up where you left off.

    On the PC, the Mac or the phone, Continue watching takes you back to the right minute. You see when it ends before you even press play.

  3. Series

    The next episode follows on its own.

    Intros are skipped in one gesture, and each series keeps its own languages: the anime in Japanese, the sitcom in English.

  4. Library

    Find anything, right away.

    Search, filters and sorting for hundreds of titles, and a library kept on disk: it opens even while the server sleeps.

  5. Playback

    The right languages, from the first episode.

    Audio tracks, subtitles at your size, chapters and previews on the seek bar: playback happens in Mira, not in a browser.

00:02 · On your phone

In your pocket, no App Store.

Mira web is served by your own Jellyfin server. On iPhone it opens like an app from the Home Screen, at home or anywhere with Tailscale. On Android too.

  1. Prepare everythingIn Mira for Windows: Guide, then Mira on your phone.
  2. Scan the QR codeThe camera opens Mira web, already at the right address.
  3. Add to Home ScreenShare, then Add to Home Screen. That’s it.

00:03 · With others

The same film, each at home.

Watch together

Make a group, send the link. Your friend opens it, and you watch at the same moment. A pause on one side pauses the other.

Together · playing in sync

No Jellyfin yet?

Mira installs and prepares it for you, then a single button sets up the rest for the phone. One Windows prompt, and it’s ready.

  1. Jellyfin installed on this PC
  2. Films, series and anime sorted
  3. Firewall open to private networks only
  4. QR code ready for your phone

00:04 · Privacy

Nothing leaves your server.

Your library, what you watch and when: it all stays between your devices and your Jellyfin.

  • No Mira accountYou sign in to your Jellyfin, and no one else.
  • Nothing collectedNo analytics, no ads, no report sent behind your back.
  • One single questionIs there an update? Asked to GitHub, and it can be turned off on Windows.
  • Nor does this siteNo cookies, no trackers, no fonts loaded from elsewhere. Hosted by GitHub Pages.
