Player

The right languages, from the first episode.

Playback happens in Mira, not in a browser: the file as it is, its audio tracks, its subtitles at your size.

  • Subtitles found for you

    Checked against the film and synced to the voices, by your server.

  • Previews on the seek bar

    Intros skipped in one gesture, the next episode on its own.

  • Sound for your amp

    Dolby and DTS sent untouched, from Mira for Windows.

Together

One link, the same film.

Send your party link. Your friends join from their phone or their computer, even from their own Jellyfin. A pause on one side pauses everyone.

Setup

No Jellyfin yet? Mira takes care of it.

On the PC that keeps your films, Mira for Windows installs Jellyfin and prepares everything in one click, with a single Windows prompt.

  • A server that sets itself up

    Firewall open to your home only, PC kept awake, graphics card, backups and updates.

  • The phone in one scan

    A QR code opens Mira web at the right address, at home or away.

  • Built-in Tailscale

    Sign in once: Mira finds your server from anywhere, on every device.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your server.

Your library, what you watch and when: it stays between your devices and your Jellyfin.

  • No Mira account

    You sign in to your Jellyfin, and no one else. No analytics, no ads.

  • Only what you switch on

    The update check, Tailscale, subtitle search: each one says what it sends before it starts.

  • Nor does this site

    No cookies, no trackers, nothing loaded from elsewhere. Hosted by GitHub Pages.
