Mira, a Jellyfin client for Windows, Mac and iPhone

Mira plays your Jellyfin library like at the movies, on Windows, Mac and iPhone . Every file plays as it is, in the right language from the first episode, and the next one  is always waiting. Send a link  and a friend watches the same film, at the same second, from anywhere. No Mira account, no analytics: nothing leaves your server.
