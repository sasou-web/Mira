Your Jellyfin library, played like at the movies.

Mira is a free client for Windows, Mac and iPhone. It plays every file as it is, remembers where you were on each screen, and lets a friend watch with you from a link.

Every file, as it is.

On Windows and Mac, mpv plays inside Mira: any format, the right audio and subtitles from the first episode. Your server converts only when it has to.

Same film, same second.

Send your party link. Your friend opens it, gives a first name and watches with you, from anywhere, without signing up.

Nothing leaves your server.

No Mira account, no analytics, no ads. Away from home, the built-in Tailscale reaches your server with nothing else to install.
